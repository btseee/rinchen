# D10 Distillation and students: design

Status: binding for the D10 plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-09-d10-distillation.md`), written before the build and AMENDED TO THE FINISHED CODE by the last task of the plan (2026-11-09, code tip `cc917e2`): sections 1 to 7 say what the code does, and section 8 lists every place the build differs from what was first written, with the reason and the evidence. The runbook is `docs/runbooks/10_distillation.md`. Master plan: `docs/research/2026-10-29-rinchen-dev-agent-prompt.md`, milestone D10. Evidence for every fact about Eole, CTranslate2 and the toy runs: `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-09-d10-distillation/spike-findings.md` (a spike on this machine, Eole 0.6.4, CTranslate2 4.8.4, CPU, toy data; scratch scripts named there). Everything GPU-side is unmeasured and labelled so.

## 1. Goal and non-goals

Goal: a pipeline from the teacher to two production students, built and proven on the CPU with tiny models: the teacher (OUR checkpoint) is exported to CTranslate2 with the GPU hidden; it translates the source side of the authentic training corpus and its beam output becomes a sequence-level knowledge-distillation (KD) corpus in the project's corpus schema, with a provenance manifest; the KD corpus is mixed with the original data by an exact integer ratio and a student of another, smaller shape (more encoder than decoder layers, its own SentencePiece model) is trained from random initialisation on the mix with the existing launcher; the student is evaluated against the teacher on the same dev rows and exported to CTranslate2. STOP gate (master plan: "a tiny student is exported"): through the real `rinchen` commands and the real Eole and CTranslate2 on the CPU, a tiny teacher (2,000 steps) is exported, its KD corpus of the toy training sources is generated, a tiny student (2 encoder and 1 decoder layers) trains for 2,000 steps on KD text mixed 1:1 with the originals, its dev chrF is at least 95.0 and at least the teacher's minus 3.0, and the student is exported to CTranslate2 (CPU), whose greedy output equals Eole's on at least 95 % of 200 dev sentences.

Non-goals:
- No GPU was used and none is claimed (the machine has one that the owner did not authorise). No real teacher, no real corpus, no real KD data exist: the toy sentences are templated and test machinery, not translation quality. Every threshold of the pipeline is an unvalidated prior; the toy gate does not rank anything (three seeds of the same student span 1.9 chrF).
- No quantisation, no `rinchen.json`, no inference engine, no serving, no `rinchen translate`, no strict CTranslate2/Eole parity (log-probabilities), no script masking: all D11. D10 builds only the export its gate needs, shaped so that D11 takes it over (T13).
- No fine-tuning of any model: a student starts from random initialisation, always (`train_from` and `reset_optim` stay unknown keys in every config; `distill prepare` renders neither). An external model is never a teacher (the teacher is a checkpoint our launcher wrote and our manifest vouches for).
- No document-level teacher (D9's continued model translates windows `<2xx> <doc> a <sep> b`): the sentence-level teacher first (T14).
- No n-best as extra targets, no monolingual KD sources, no `<ft>` at inference, no QE scorer run (the real scorer needs a GPU): recorded as open questions (section 7).
- No FLORES+ in D10: the evaluation reads the corpus's own `dev` rows; no `test` row is read by any D10 code.

## 2. Findings that bind the design (spike)

1. A teacher exports to CTranslate2 with `export_ct2` (D5's function, moved by D10 from `script_conv.export` to `rinchen.export.eole_ct2`; the GPU hidden: D-CT2-EOLE-CUDA) in 2 to 5 s for the tiny model; the export is `model.bin`, `shared_vocabulary.json` (the model's whole padded vocabulary in Eole's order, which depends on the training data, with all 118 reserved symbols present thanks to the D9 invariant and a dummy padding token at the end) and a `config.json` of 223 bytes that names no layer count, no tag, no tokenizer. CTranslate2 greedy equals `eole predict` greedy on 200 of 200 dev sentences for a source-tag teacher, a target-tag teacher and the student; a batch gives what single sentences give.
2. Source tag (`src_prefix`): CTranslate2 source `[<2xx>, *pieces]`, no `target_prefix`, no BOS or EOS. Target tag (`tgt_prefix`): source `pieces`, `target_prefix=[[<2xx>]]`, and the hypotheses START with the prefix token. An unknown tag is silent (`<unk>`, garbled output). `return_end_token=True` makes a finished hypothesis end with `</s>` and a cut one not, for greedy and for beam search; `max_decoding_length` (default 256) and `max_input_length` (default 1024, the source) cut silently.
3. `num_hypotheses <= beam_size * patience` (D-CT2-NUMHYP). The hypotheses of the beam are distinct and sorted; on the toy ranks 2 to 4 are about 41 chrF below rank 1. D6's selection (MBR over the survivors) lowered the agreement of the KD targets with the references from 99.6 % (top-1, n=1) to 76.4 % (n=4) on the toy teacher's own training sources.
4. D6's `Translator`, `plan_shards`, `ShardStore`, `select_candidates` and `build_rows` run over a CTranslate2 teacher as they are. `tags.origin_for` knew no generator kind that gives the origin `ft` (the origin "reserved for forward translation by our own teacher"; D10 added the kind `teacher`, T2 and section 8), and D6's job fingerprint omits the weights behind a model path.
5. `mixture.load_training_rows` EXCLUDES the origin `ft` (and `synth_llm`, `synth_nmt`) from training: a KD corpus never enters an ablation by accident; D10 reads it through its own loader.
6. Eole drops, silently, a training example whose tokenised source equals one already in the same minibatch. KD rows that repeat an original source (always, for KD of the training sources) collided: 5.19 % of all examples dropped when the KD corpus was mixed 1:1 untagged, 0 % with the origin tag `<ft>` on the KD rows (the D7 convention of `<bt>`: after the direction tag for placement `src`, alone as `src_prefix` for placement `tgt`), 0 % for the originals alone. The toy KD target equals the authentic one for 99.6 % of the rows, which puts the two rows side by side in the id-sorted files; real data will differ (unmeasured).
7. Encoder and decoder depth are keys the config allow-lists already hold; Eole, `launch` and CTranslate2 accept a 2/1 shape. A 3/1 shape at the 2/1 recipe did not converge in 2,000 steps (dev chrF 38 against 98.6): depth needs its own recipe, unmeasured for the real shapes.
8. A student with another SentencePiece model trains on KD text unchanged (KD is text; Eole's `sentencepiece` transform cuts it with the student's model). What depends on the model: the pre-tokenised validation corpus, `src_vocab_size` (pieces + 1), the evaluation (each model its own). Student dev chrF with a 500-piece model was 89.4 against 98.6 with the 640-piece one (the coarser model cuts nouns into characters).
9. `evaluate_variant` evaluates any checkpoint of ours with its own SentencePiece model. `continuation.verify_source` vouches for a final step or an averaged directory (manifest and hashes) and returns the hashes; the checkpoint directory also holds a copy of the SentencePiece model that the manifest does not vouch for.
10. Toy numbers (single seed unless said): teacher 2/2 d64 2,000 steps dev chrF 98.20; student 2/1 d64 KD 1:1 tagged 98.58 (seeds 1234, 7, 99: 98.58, 98.59, 96.68); originals only 97.01. Parameters (formula of runbook 08 calibrated exactly on the two tiny checkpoints, ARITHMETIC): teacher about 1,103 M, server student about 190.8 M, edge student about 79.0 M.

## 3. Rulings

T1. Teacher serving. The teacher is a checkpoint of ours (the final step of a run `rinchen train launch` finished, or a directory `rinchen train average` wrote) whose manifest vouches for its files (`continuation.verify_source`; its refusal text says "cannot continue from", a wording debt left as it is). `rinchen distill export` turns it into a directory: the three converter files, `spm.model` (a copy of the SentencePiece model, whose SHA-256 must equal that of the copy Eole saved into the checkpoint directory, the file the saved `config.json` names under `${MODEL_PATH}`), `export.json` (the record: tag placement, the checkpoint's kind, step and SHA-256 of weights, `config.json`, `vocab.json` and manifest, the SentencePiece SHA-256, encoder and decoder layers, hidden size, vocabulary entries) and, last, `manifest.json` (stage `distill_export`) with every file's SHA-256. The converter is `rinchen.export.eole_ct2.export_ct2`, the D5 function MOVED out of `script_conv.export` (the layering test forbids `train` to import `script_conv`; `script_conv.export` re-exports the three names, behaviour unchanged). The tag placement is not in CTranslate2's files, but it IS in the saved `config.json` of a checkpoint our launcher wrote (the `src_prefix` and `tgt_prefix` of its `data` section, read by `train/distill_saved_config.py`): `--tag-placement` is an explicit, required option, recorded in `export.json`, and it is REFUSED (`ConfigError`, before the converter starts) when the saved config places the direction tag in the other place; a saved config without a `data` section or without a direction tag leaves the statement unchecked, and the record does not say whether the placement was derived or stated. The converter refuses an output directory that exists, so it writes into a child of a private directory `.<name>.<random>` made beside `--out-dir`, which is renamed onto `--out-dir` when the export is complete; its log stays beside the output (`<out>.export.log`); the record's `vocabulary_entries` is checked against the converter's `shared_vocabulary.json`.
T2. The teacher as a `Translator` (`train/distill_teacher.py`, CTranslate2 behind the D6 protocol). `TeacherTranslator` loads a verified export (`verify_export`: every file's SHA-256 against `export.json` and the manifest, a foreign or missing manifest refused, hashes taken once) and translates with `translate_batch(beam_size, num_hypotheses=n, max_decoding_length, max_batch_size, return_end_token=True)`, on `device` `cpu` (default) or `cuda` (an explicit operator choice; never `auto`, which would pick the GPU), `compute_type` as given, `intra_threads` `threads`. Source tokens by placement (finding 2). Pre-flight (`check_directions`): every direction tag is an entry of `shared_vocabulary.json` AND one piece of the SentencePiece model, else `ConfigError` naming the tag (finding 2: silent otherwise). Per text: a source with no piece gets no candidates and is counted (`empty_source`); a source of more than `max_source_pieces` pieces (default 256, at most 1,023: CTranslate2's own limit is 1,024 and silent) gets NO candidates and is counted (`source_too_long`); a hypothesis is dropped and counted under the FIRST reason that applies: no end token (`truncated`), then a piece no translation holds (`reserved_symbol`: the unknown piece of the SentencePiece model, the end token in the middle, any of the 118 project symbols, or any entry of `shared_vocabulary.json` that is no piece of the model, such as Eole's `<blank>` and its padding tokens; the pieces of a byte-fallback model stay; decided BEFORE the hypothesis is decoded), then a line break (`mixture.LINE_BREAK_CHARACTERS`, `line_break`); candidates are cleaned as D6's (`clean_candidates`). `n <= beam_size`. The counters (six keys: `sources`, `source_too_long`, `empty_source`, `truncated`, `line_break`, `reserved_symbol`) cover the texts translated by THIS process (a shard reused from disk adds nothing: reported, never hidden). The file reads of the translator go through `train/distill_export_files.py` (only the bytes that were hashed). The source-token function is pure and separate (`ct2_source_tokens`) so that D11's engine can take it.
T3. KD sources. The sources are the `src` texts of the AUTHENTIC training rows of the corpus files, through D7's `load_training_rows` (split `train`, no `filter_reason`, group `auth`: so no dev, test or holdout row, no back-translated and no `ft` row is read as a source), one source per direction and text, in registry directions only, sorted by id; the id is `make_row_id(src_lang, tgt_lang, src, "")`. Optional `--direction` restricts to some. The authentic targets of a source are kept to count how many KD targets equal one of them (`matches_reference`, a diagnostic, never a filter).
T4. KD generation (`train/distill_generate.py`). Per direction (sorted), `plan_shards(frame, shard_size)` (D6, default 512), the shards of ALL directions in one directory `shards-<fingerprint[:12]>/<direction>/` managed by `ShardStore`; a shard with a valid file is reused, else translated and written before the next, so a crash resumes; selection per shard with `select_candidates(kind="ft", synthetic_lang=<tgt_lang>, cfg=SelectionConfig, scorer)` (empty, script purity, copy, optional QE, MBR among several survivors; a lone survivor wins unscored) and `build_rows(kind="ft", generator_kind="teacher", generator_name=<name>)`. The fingerprint is `sha256` over canonical JSON of: the teacher's identity (SHA-256 of `model.bin`, `shared_vocabulary.json`, `config.json` and `spm.model`, the tag placement), the decoding options that decide a hypothesis (`beam_size`, `n`, `max_decoding_length`, `max_source_pieces`, `device`, `compute_type`; NOT the batch size or the thread count), `shard_size`, `seed` (it names the directory; decoding is deterministic), and per direction the `(id, text)` rows in id order. The selection thresholds are not in it (a rerun with other thresholds reuses the shards, as D6). `force` ignores the shards. A translator that raises, returns the wrong number of lists or a non-list is an error naming the shard; the shards written so far stay. A source with no letter or digit (whitespace, punctuation, zero-width characters) is not sent to the teacher: it gets no candidates and is counted in `no_candidates` and in `letterless_sources`. The step of one shard (`shard_candidates`: read back, or asked of the teacher and written) is `train/distill_shards.py`; `generate_kd` sorts the sources by direction itself.
T5. The KD corpus row. A frame in `CORPUS_SCHEMA` (`corpus.schema`), written with `write_corpus`: `origin` `ft`, `corpus` `ft:<name>` (`<name>` is `--name` or `rinchen-<first 12 hex of the SHA-256 of model.bin>`, `[A-Za-z0-9._-]{1,64}`), `split` `train`, `src_lang`/`tgt_lang`/`src`/`tgt` the pair (the real source, the teacher's text), `scores.script_tgt` the purity of the target and `scores.metricx_qe` or `scores.cometkiwi` the QE score when a scorer ran, `doc_id`, `seg_idx` and `filter_reason` null, one `created` stamp, `id` = `make_row_id` of the pair. One row per source that kept a target (top-1 by default, T6); a source without one is counted under its reason. Rows are in direction order, then source id order. Files under `--out-dir`: `kd.parquet`, `counts.json` (fingerprint, the corpus name, the seed, teacher identity, decoding, selection, counts by direction and in total with every key of D6's `COUNT_KEYS`, `letterless_sources`, the teacher's counters and the note of what they cover, `matches_reference`, shard statistics, QE status), `shards-<fingerprint[:12]>/...` (work files, not outputs) and `manifest.json`, last: stage `distill_generate`, inputs the corpus files, the selection config, `spm.model`, `model.bin`, `shared_vocabulary.json`, `config.json`, `export.json` and the export manifest, each hashed before it was read; outputs `kd.parquet` (rows) and `counts.json`; metrics the counts (dotted: `count.<direction>.<name>`, `count.total.<name>`), `matches_reference.*`, `letterless_sources.*`, the shard statistics, `teacher.<counter>` and `decoding.*`; `seed` the `--seed`; status `generate: ok`, `qe: ok | skipped: ...`, `teacher.name` (the KD CORPUS name, not a name of the teacher), `teacher.tag_placement`, `teacher.checkpoint_weights_sha256`, `teacher.checkpoint_manifest_sha256`, `teacher.model_sha256`, `decoding` (the options as JSON text).
T6. n-best. Default `n=1` with beam 4: the KD target of a source is the beam's top hypothesis (finding 3). `--n` up to the beam (D-CT2-NUMHYP) keeps the n hypotheses in the shards and lets the D6 selection pick by MBR; the manifest records `n`. Several rows per source (n-best as extra targets) are NOT built: the toy's ranks 2 to 4 are 41 chrF below rank 1 and the schema would need a rank (it has none).
T7. QE. Optional (`--qe`, off by default: the real scorer needs a GPU and was never loaded here). With it, the D6 QE floor of `SelectionConfig.qe` runs on the pairs (real source, candidate) in the `ft` orientation; the CLI refuses (`ConfigError`) when `qe.skip_reason` says the scorer cannot run, never skipping silently (an operator who asked for the filter must not get an unfiltered corpus), and releases the scorer afterwards. Tests inject `tests.support.planted_synth.FakeScorer`.
T8. Mixing by an exact integer ratio (`train/distill_mix.py`). `--mix K:O` (default `1:1`, two positive integers, reduced by their greatest common divisor) means that in EVERY direction exactly K/(K+O) of the examples Eole draws come from the KD corpus. Eole's corpus `weight` is the number of consecutive examples per round of a cycle (D-EOLE-WEIGHT-SEMANTICS), so the share is the weight over the sum whatever the corpus sizes. The original corpora (authentic and back-translated, `build_corpora`, `--bt-ratio`) keep D7's mixing (temperature over the directions' original rows, `mixing_probabilities`, `integer_weights`, `--weight-total`); then every original weight is multiplied by O and the KD corpus of direction d gets `K x (the sum of d's original weights)`. So `weight_kd / sum(original weights of d) = K / O` exactly, and the shares of the directions are the temperature's, unchanged. A direction WITHOUT a KD corpus (`generate --direction` covered only some directions) gets its originals scaled by `K + O` (the reduced numbers) instead of `O`, so that its share of the draws stays the temperature's too (controller ruling, section 8). The weights sum to `(K + O) x weight_total`. Per direction `kd.repeat.<direction>` (how many times more often a KD row than an original row is drawn) is recorded, never gated. A KD corpus is never subsampled or upsampled; a KD row of a direction that has no original corpus is not read for training (counted under `unknown_direction` of the KD statistics).
T9. The KD tag. KD corpora are named `<direction>.kd` and carry the origin tag `<ft>` (`synth.tags.data_origin_tag("ft")`) in `src_prefix`: `<2xx> <ft>` for placement `src`, `<ft>` alone (with `tgt_prefix` the direction tag) for `tgt`, by an optional keyword of `eole_data.data_section` (default on; no corpus of group `kd` exists in D7, so D7 is unchanged). `--no-kd-tag` leaves the KD rows untagged: allowed, recorded (`kd_tag: 0` in the manifest) and measured to lose rows to Eole's deduplication on the toy (finding 6). At inference the student is used WITHOUT `<ft>` (the toy measured both alike: 98.58 and 98.55); whether `<ft>` helps a real student is open.
T10. The student configs. `configs/eole/student_server.yaml` (encoder 16, decoder 3, hidden 768, heads 12, feed-forward 3072, word vector size 768, `SinusoidalInterleaved`, shared embeddings, `src_vocab_size: 64001` = the 64,000 pieces of `configs/spm/student_64k.yaml` + Eole's pad token), `student_edge.yaml` (encoder 12, decoder 2, hidden 512, heads 8, feed-forward 2048, word vector size 512, the same vocabulary size) and `student_tiny.yaml` (encoder 2, decoder 1, hidden 64, heads 4, feed-forward 128, the CPU recipe of `teacher_tiny.yaml`, 2,000 steps), all with `data: {}` and no per-run value. Server and edge hold the teacher's schedule scaled to the model, with one stated difference (`attention_dropout: [0.1]`, which `teacher.yaml` does not state); they are unmeasured priors, said in each file's header with the formula-based parameter counts; no code changes are needed for the depths (finding 7). A student's vocabulary is the vocabulary invariant of D9 (every reserved symbol present) applied to the student's own SentencePiece model.
T11. Preparing a student run (`train/distill_prepare.py`; trains nothing). It reads the original corpus files (`load_training_rows`, `load_dev_rows` of D7), the KD Parquet files (`distill_corpus.load_kd_rows(paths, *, directions, digests=None)`: each KD file must have a `manifest.json` beside it of stage `distill_generate`, status ok, listing it with the SHA-256 it has now (the SHA-256 the command took before it read anything, when it gives one: hash once); every KD row is counted once, in the kept rows or under a reason, in this order: `not_train`, `filtered`, `wrong_origin` (not `ft`), `empty`, `line_break`, `unknown_direction` (no original corpus for its direction)), writes the original corpora (D7's files), the KD corpora (`<out>/data/kd/<direction>.kd.src/.tgt/.json` through `write_corpus_files`, reused when unchanged), the validation corpus (the dev rows of the training directions, pre-tokenised with the STUDENT's SentencePiece model, finding 8) and the config (the student base with `src_vocab`, `data`, `transforms_configs.sentencepiece`, `training.model_path`, `seed` and `src_vocab_size` laid over it: the stated size is kept when it holds every piece of the student's model + 1 and refused when smaller, set to pieces + 1 when absent; the base must hold no corpora, no `train_from`, no `reset_optim`). `prepare_student` takes `kd_digests` and `before_writing` (called once, after every refusal and before the first write, never in a dry run: the command makes the output directory and removes the old manifest there). Layout as D9's `docify`: `<out>/config.yaml` with its run files `<out>/run/vocab.src` and `<out>/run/ckpt`, so that `rinchen train launch --config <out>/config.yaml --run-dir <out>/run` trains the student from random initialisation (resumable as any D8 run). Manifest `distill_prepare`, last: inputs the corpus files, the KD files and their manifests, the SentencePiece model and the base config; outputs the corpora, the validation files and the config; metrics D7's counts (`count.*`), the KD counts (`kd.count.*`), `mix.kd`, `mix.original`, `weight.<corpus>`, `kd.repeat.<direction>`, `kd_tag`, `bt_ratio`, `temperature`, `weight_total`, `valid.lines`; status `prepare: ok`, `tag_placement`, `mix`.
T12. Evaluation. `rinchen distill evaluate` runs D7's `evaluate_variant` on one checkpoint (a final step directory, an averaged directory or a run's `ckpt`) with its own SentencePiece model and placement over the `dev` rows of the corpus files (directions of the training rows, as the ablation does), prints and writes `result.json` (`DevResult`: chrF, BLEU, tag leaks and signatures per direction, and `checkpoint`, `spm_sha256`, `placement`, `max_lines`; sorted keys, LF, the same inputs give the same bytes) and the hypotheses, with a manifest `distill_evaluate`. Its `--tag-placement` is checked against the checkpoint's saved config as `export`'s is (a disagreement is exit 2; a saved config that is not what Eole writes is exit 3). Teacher against student is two runs with the same corpus and `--max-lines`; the same dev rows give comparable numbers. No test row is read.
T13. Student export, and what D11 takes over. `rinchen distill export` is T1's command for any checkpoint of ours; the gate exports the student's final step. D10 builds only: `export/eole_ct2.py` (the converter call), `train/distill_export.py` (the provenance checks, the record, `verify_export`) and the command. D11 builds `export/ct2.py::convert(eole_ckpt, out, quantization)` around `export_ct2` (quantisation `int8` and `int8_float16`), `rinchen.json` (languages, tags, version) in place of `export.json`, the engine that reads an export (`verify_export` is its reader), `rinchen translate`, serving, the strict parity gate and the quantisation gate. The gate's CTranslate2 smoke translation is a number to record with a 95 % floor (the figure D11 states), not D11's gate.
T14. The document-level teacher is optional and not built: it would translate windows `[<2xx>, <doc>, *pieces]` joined with `<sep>` (D9) and split the output with `infer.doc_chunker.split_output`; `ct2_source_tokens` is the one place a window format would enter, and `infer/` stays free of `train` and `cli`.
T15. Resumability and provenance. Generation resumes by shard (T4); `prepare` is idempotent (unchanged corpus files are reused; the manifest says how many); training resumes by `launch` (D8); `export` refuses an existing output directory (the converter does). Every command hashes its inputs ONCE, before it reads them (hash once: the digest is passed to whatever vouches for the file, never taken again), refuses a foreign manifest in its output directory (`refuse_foreign_manifest`) and an output directory that is an input's, creates its output directory after every refusal, writes its manifest last (`begin_commit`; `export` makes a brand-new directory by a rename and has none) and exits as D9's commands do (0; 2 bad option, config or provenance; 3 unreadable or empty data; 4 a tool failed; 1 cannot write). The chain of hashes: the teacher's `train_launch` or `train_average` manifest -> `export.json` and its manifest -> the KD manifest -> the prepare manifest (KD files by hash) -> the student's `train_launch` manifest (the config by hash) -> the student's export record.
T16. The GPU. Every Eole and CTranslate2 run of D10's tests and measurements has `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=""` and the device `cpu`; the converter runs through `export_environment()`; `--device cuda` exists for the operator and is unmeasured.

## 7. Open questions, recorded as such

- Everything GPU-side, every real corpus and the real teacher are unmeasured: the throughput of CTranslate2 on a 1.1 B-parameter teacher (CPU, CUDA, float16, int8), the memory of a KD run, the time to hash a real `model.bin`, the real students' training (speed, convergence, learning rate, batch, steps), the real mix ratio, and whether a deeper encoder than the 2/1 toy shape trains at the teacher's recipe (3/1 did not at the tiny one).
- The quality of the teacher's n-best on real data, whether MBR over it helps (it hurt on the toy), whether the lower ranks are useful extra targets, and the thresholds of the selection (script share 0.9, QE floor 8.0) are unvalidated priors; the real QE scorer was not run.
- Whether Eole's deduplication drops a meaningful share of untagged KD rows on real data (5.2 % on the toy) and whether `<ft>` at inference helps or hurts a student; the default (tag in training, none at inference) is measured on the toy only.
- Monolingual KD sources (D6's monolingual frames have the shape the generator takes; no option exists), KD of the bt-group sources, a document-level teacher, KD from several teachers (the loader merges KD files per direction and records the corpus labels), and the `tgt` placement for a real student (covered by unit tests, never trained).
- The counters of the teacher cover the shards generated by the process that reports them; a resumed run reports less (and the manifest key `teacher.name` holds the KD corpus name). `verify_source`'s refusals say "cannot continue from". The CTranslate2 smoke floor (95 %) is D11's figure, measured here only on three tiny models. The limits the review left open (an empty selection exits 0, "exact" means exact weights, memory and scale, no collision rate printed for `tgt`) are listed in the runbook's "Known limits".

## 8. Amendments made while building

Where the finished code (tip `233ba33`) differs from what sections 1 to 7 first said, why, and the evidence. The evidence is in the ledger (`.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-09-d10-distillation/progress.md`, `fix-wave-1.md`), the commits named, `docs/runbooks/10_distillation.md` and `docs/VERIFY.md` ("D10 findings").

**A1. The tag placement IS in the checkpoint and is checked (T1, spike finding F1; review I1, `95a28c8`).** The spike and T1 said the placement "is not in the checkpoint". That is false for a checkpoint our launcher wrote: the saved `config.json` keeps the `data` section with each corpus's `src_prefix` and `tgt_prefix`. The review's end-to-end failure: a source-tag teacher exported with `--tag-placement tgt` exited 0 with a vouching manifest, and `generate` over it gave 1,190 KD rows of which 1 matched an authentic target, with Cyrillic garbage that passed the script rule. `train/distill_saved_config.py` reads the placement back (a whole-word direction tag in `src_prefix` is `src`, in `tgt_prefix` is `tgt`; `<bt>`, `<ft>` and `<doc>` tell nothing) from the verified bytes of the saved config and `distill export` refuses a statement that disagrees (exit 2, before the converter starts; nothing made); `distill evaluate` checks the same way. A saved config without a `data` section or a direction tag leaves the statement as it is; contradictory saved data (a tag on both sides, corpora that disagree) is a `DataError`, exit 3. The record is UNCHANGED: it does not say whether the placement was derived or stated (a schema bump would refuse every existing record), and an export made before the check cannot be re-verified (the saved config is not in the export directory: re-export is the remedy). The re-review's minor 4 added the report (A12): the run says which it was. Real artifacts, by the fix wave: source-tag teacher + `tgt` exit 2, + `src` exit 0; target-tag teacher + `src` exit 2; student + `tgt` exit 2; and for this runbook the averaged tiny teacher + `tgt` exit 2 in 1.3 s, `evaluate` the same in 1 s.

**A2. The export staging layout (T1, T13; review M1, `6eb778a`).** T1 said the converter writes `out_dir`. The converter refuses an existing directory, and the first version removed whatever sat at `out_dir` after a failure, which could be a directory another process made after the last check. Now the converter writes into a child of a private directory `.<name>.<random>` made beside `out_dir` (`tempfile.mkdtemp`), the two extra files are written there, and the child is renamed onto `out_dir` after one more existence check; a failure removes the private directory, and `out_dir` only if this call put it in place. A `rename` onto an empty directory that appears in the last instant would replace it (stated in the code, left); a hard kill (SIGKILL, an out-of-memory kill, power loss) leaves the private directory behind, as large as the model, safe to delete when no export is running and swept by nothing (SIGINT and SIGTERM clean it up: the re-review's probe; A12). `export_ct2` gained an optional `log_path` (default unchanged) so that the log stays beside the output as `<out>.export.log`.

**A3. A vocabulary cross-check and a stricter reader (T1; Task 1, review M2).** The record's `vocabulary_entries` is compared with the converter's `shared_vocabulary.json` at export time (`UpstreamError`, the private directory removed) and in `verify_export` (when the file is a list of strings). A saved config naming two different SentencePiece models for the two sides is refused. The docstring says that the converter reads the weights AFTER the digest was taken (a TOCTOU window, as for every input). Task 1 was stricter than the text in four more places: glob characters on `out_dir` are refused (`D-EOLE-GLOB-CKPT`), the converter must have written `shared_vocabulary.json` and `config.json` too (`UpstreamError`, the output removed), `ExportRecord.from_bytes` refuses non-positive counts, digests that are not 64 hex characters, a kind that disagrees with its step and a repeated JSON key, and an invalid tag placement is a `ValueError`. The converter move required one edit to an existing test, `tests/integration/test_script_conv_tiny.py` (the CUDA probe patches `rinchen.export.eole_ct2.export_environment`).

**A4. Five counters, then six (T2; Task 2 and review I2, `fee9449`, `46c160e`, `cc917e2`).** T2 named three counters. Task 2 added `empty_source` (a source with no piece is not sent and gets `[]`; `translate` also runs the tag checks for empty texts), and the review's I2 added `reserved_symbol`: a hypothesis holding the unknown piece, the end token in the middle, a reserved symbol or a direction tag decoded to literal text (the unknown piece decodes to U+2047 between spaces) and passed D6's selection with `dropped_purity 0`, so a student would learn to emit it. The counter reaches `counts.json`, the manifest metrics and the result line. The second commit added the vocabulary entries that are no piece of the SentencePiece model (Eole's `<blank>`, the padding tokens such as `averyunlikelytoken0`), which decode to their letters; byte-fallback pieces stay. A hypothesis is counted under the first reason: truncated, reserved symbol, line break. Real check: 800 real hypotheses of the spike's teacher gave `reserved_symbol` 0, so the rule has no real positive. Other Task 2 deviations: constructor errors map `ValueError` to `ConfigError` and `OSError` or `RuntimeError` to `DataError`; the hypothesis's line-break check is before `strip`; the file reads moved to `distill_export_files.py` and the tests into three files.

**A5. Letterless sources, the shard module, sorted sources (T3, T4; Task 3, `8707c9a`).** A source with no letter or digit is not sent to the teacher (it would invent a sentence for a bare zero-width character, which D7's empty rule, `str.strip`, does not catch): it gets no candidates and is counted in `no_candidates` and `letterless_sources`. `generate_kd` sorts the sources by direction itself and refuses an empty source list, `shard_size < 1` and a bad name. The shard step is `train/distill_shards.py` (an extra module to keep `distill_generate.py` under 450 lines; about 40 lines of D6's reply validation are re-implemented because D6's helpers are private). The fingerprint recipe dropped redundant entries (`src_lang`, `tgt_lang`, the row count: the direction and the rows already say them; the golden value changed once, before the commit).

**A6. Partial KD coverage (T8; Task 4, `308a1cc`; the controller's ruling).** T8 scaled every original corpus by `O`. With `generate --direction` covering only some directions that makes the directions WITHOUT a KD corpus weigh `O x S` against `(K + O) x S` for the covered ones, so their share of the draws would drop below the temperature's. Ruling: the originals of a direction without KD are scaled by `K + O` (reduced numbers). For full coverage the result is T8's; the weights add up to `(K + O) x` the sum of D7's. Pinned by unit and property tests; probed through the real CLI by the review (partial coverage 33, 66, 34, 34 and KD 33, sum 200; a 3:2 mix giving KD 99 against originals 66; a back-translated mix at 2:1 with KD 62 = 2 x 31 and 38 = 2 x 19) and again for this runbook (the partial-coverage example and a table of 1:1, 3:1, 3:2 and 2:1). Stricter than T8: `parse_mix` refuses leading zeros and digit strings above 10,000 (`MAX_MIX_TERM`), `mixed_weights` refuses two KD corpora in one direction, `load_kd_rows` matches the manifest entry by file name, `prepare_student` makes `out_dir` and the SentencePiece path absolute and refuses `$` and glob characters before the first write. The KD rows of an unknown direction are `unknown_direction` in `KdStats`.

**A7. The CLI (T11, T12, T15; Tasks 5 and 6, `7a322b8`, `7022cfe`).** The generate command is three modules (`distill_generate.py`, `distill_generate_plan.py`, `distill_generate_records.py`) to stay under 450 lines. `export` hashes its five outputs with `output_file` (its inputs use the record's digests, never hashed again), makes no `begin_commit` (its directory is brand new) and removes its directory when the manifest write fails or on interrupt. `generate` runs `begin_commit` AFTER every preflight and BEFORE `generate_kd` (a crash leaves no manifest and the earlier `kd.parquet` untouched; D6's synth command keeps the old manifest during generation), lists the selection config as a manifest input (its `config_sha256`), hashes the inputs and verifies the export in a dry run but builds no teacher (so a dry run cannot check tags), checks `require_out_apart_from_inputs` before `refuse_foreign_manifest` (an output directory equal to the teacher's is exit 2), takes numeric options with `min=1`, has `--device` as `cpu | cuda` (`auto` is a usage error), and builds and vets the QE scorer before the output directory is made.
