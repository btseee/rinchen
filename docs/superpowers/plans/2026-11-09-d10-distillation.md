# D10 Distillation and Students Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build sequence-level knowledge distillation from OUR teacher to smaller students: the CTranslate2 export of a checkpoint of ours with a provenance record, the teacher as a `Translator` behind D6's generator seam, the KD corpus generation (shards, fingerprint, selection, corpus rows, manifest), the exact-ratio mix of KD and original data and the preparation of a student run (a student config of another depth and its own SentencePiece model), the `rinchen distill` commands, and a tiny student that is trained from random init on KD text, evaluated against the teacher and exported to CTranslate2 (the STOP gate).

**Architecture:** `export.eole_ct2` holds the converter call (moved from D5); `train/distill_export` verifies a checkpoint's provenance and writes/reads an export directory; `train/distill_teacher` is the CTranslate2 translator; `train/distill_sources` + `train/distill_generate` produce the KD corpus with D6's shard store and selection; `train/distill_corpus` + `distill_mix` + `distill_prepare` turn it into a student run; `cli/distill*` are the commands; training is D8's `rinchen train launch` unchanged.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.14.10, Eole 0.6.4 (subprocess only), CTranslate2 4.8.4 (Python API, CPU), sentencepiece, polars, pydantic v2, typer, pytest + hypothesis.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-d10-distillation-design.md` (binding; findings 1-10, rulings T1-T16). Spike evidence: `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-09-d10-distillation/spike-findings.md` (scratch scripts and working configs named there; read it first: every Eole and CTranslate2 fact the code relies on is there with its evidence). ## Open questions, recorded as such

What the toy cannot answer, and where the code leaves room:

1. **The real teacher's n-best.** On the toy, ranks 2 to 4 are about 41 chrF below rank 1 and MBR over them lowered the agreement with the references (99.6 % to 76.4 %). Whether a 1.1 B teacher's beam gives useful alternatives, whether MBR or QE-reranking beats top-1, and whether lower ranks make good extra targets are unmeasured. `--n` keeps the whole n-best in the shards, so a rerun with another selection reuses them; extra targets would need a rank column the corpus schema lacks.
2. **QE thresholds.** `SelectionConfig` (script share 0.9, MetricX-QE floor 8.0) are D4/D6 priors; the real scorer was never loaded (it needs a GPU) and no KD output of a real teacher has been scored.
3. **The mix ratio.** `1:1` is the default and the only ratio the toy ran; the exact-ratio machinery is tested for any K:O, the effect of K:O on a real student, and the repeat factor that is too high, are unmeasured. `kd.repeat.<direction>` is recorded, never gated.
4. **Student sizes against speed and quality.** The shapes (16/3 d768, 12/2 d512) are the master plan's; their parameter counts are ARITHMETIC; their training recipe (learning rate, batch, steps), their convergence at depth (a 3/1 tiny shape did not converge at the 2/1 recipe), their decoding speed on the GPU and on the CPU, and the int8 quality are unmeasured (speed and quantisation belong to D11 and D13).
5. **The `<ft>` tag.** Tagged KD removed Eole's deduplication losses on the toy and cost nothing measurable; whether the tag helps or hurts a real student, at inference or not, whether the deduplication share on real data is large enough to matter untagged, are unmeasured; `--no-kd-tag` exists for that experiment.
6. **A document-level teacher, monolingual KD sources, several teachers, the `tgt` placement for a real student.** Not built; the extension points are `ct2_source_tokens`, `KdSources`/`kd_sources`, the loader that merges KD files per direction, and `eole_data.data_section`'s `placement`.
7. **CTranslate2 on a GPU, in float16 or int8, and the export of a 1.1 B teacher.** Nothing here ran on a device other than the CPU; `--device cuda` and `--compute-type` are passed through and recorded, unmeasured; D11 owns the strict parity and the quantisation gates.
8. **Resumed runs report fewer teacher counters**, and `verify_source` refuses with "cannot continue from": both are recorded limits, not fixed.
