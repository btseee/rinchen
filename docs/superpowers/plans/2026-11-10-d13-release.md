## Open questions, recorded as such

1. **Real data and baselines.** The real FLORES+ dataset (id, configurations, columns, splits, sizes, document ids), NTREX (whether it offers Khalkha), the Mongolia-sourced human sets, NLLB, MADLAD and Hy-MT2 were not available: everything is exercised on the toy, on fixtures, on a real CTranslate2 directory standing in for a baseline (negative control of the vocabulary guard) and on fakes (spec section 6, items 1, 3, 4).
2. **Neural metrics.** COMET-22, MetricX-24 and MetricX-24-QE exist only as a seam over score files; B1 and B2 are unowned (T8).
3. **Tokenizers.** `char` BLEU for Japanese and Korean stands in for the missing MeCab tokenizers and the unbuilt B3 and B4; chrF++ is degenerate for languages written without spaces.
4. **Statistics.** The segment bootstrap ignores sentence correlation; the document bootstrap needs a document column that real FLORES+ rows may not map; no multiple-comparison correction; the BLEU C-library exposure across machines (golden floats at 1e-9).
5. **Contamination.** No check that training data excludes benchmark sentences; only a structural guard that code cannot read devtest.
6. **Off-target rate** is unmeasured (no language identification).
7. **The Hub.** The front matter's acceptance, a real upload, repository visibility and the workflow on GitHub were not run; `--upload` was never exercised against the real library beyond the offline adapter test.
8. **Zero-shot directions** are listed `not_trained` and never scored.
9. **Cost.** The gate adds the toy-based modules (about 4 to 8 minutes beyond the D12 fixtures it reuses) to the full gate; a real 16-direction run's cost is unmeasured.
10. **D14 and D15.** D15 decides about a `train`-split fixture suite and the tiny scorers; D14 owns the cadence of the golden parity reruns.
